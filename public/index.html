THE RULEBOOK

Ludo, but unhinged.

01 / Gather your crew
2–8 players join with a 6-character invite code. Each player has three tokens and a different starting space.

02 / Roll two dice
Each turn, two dice roll. You choose die 1, die 2 or their combined total, then click one of your highlighted tokens. To leave base, your selected dice must include a 6. Finish all three tokens to win.

03 / Doubles = power
Roll matching numbers and select one: extra turn, 25-second shield for active tokens, a one-use token lifeline, +2 movement, or clear danger warnings.

04 / Danger zones
Red spaces are hazardous. After landing there, you have your next two personal turns to move that token away. If you don't, it returns to base. Capturing an opponent on a non-safe square also sends them back.

05 / Chaos reset
At the host-selected time interval, each team's leading active token is targeted and sent home, unless protected by a shield or lifeline.

06 / The grand reversal
Once any player has finished two of their three tokens, reverse mode triggers for eight turns. Tokens then travel backwards. After that, normal play resumes.

07 / No stalling
Turns last 45 seconds. If no legal move exists, pass. Shields block losses while active; lifelines block one loss. If disconnected, you can reconnect by refreshing the same tab.
